Stigma Surrounding Mental Health

The stigma associated with mental health remains one of the biggest barriers preventing individuals from speaking up and seeking help. This stigma, both external and internal, creates a culture of silence, leaving many employees to struggle alone. Organizations often remain unaware of the emotional struggles their workforce faces, further compounding the issue.
